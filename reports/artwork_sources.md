# Mango Loco artwork and reference notes

## Chosen variant

The supplied [user reference](../assets/references/user_reference.png) is a
2200 × 2200 front product render. Its visible front says **MANGO LOCO** and
**JUICE**. The project uses the US Juice presentation and a 473 ml can envelope
per the production lead's regional choice. Crown lists the standard 16 oz / 473
ml format at 66 mm diameter × 157.23 mm height:
<https://www.crowncork.com/beverage-packaging/products/beverage-cans/16oz-473ml-standard>.
The official US page describes the 16 fl oz Juice product:
<https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-us/energy-drinks/juice-monster/mango-loco/>.
The public product page describes a 500 ml **Juiced** presentation and explicitly
notes that the pictured can may vary by region:
<https://www.monsterenergy.com/en-ie/energy-drinks/juiced-monster/mango-loco/>.
That Irish/UK edition was not used to infer the US back label.

## Public reference views checked

- Monster Energy's US product page provides an official product reference for
  the JUICE/16 fl oz variant (link above). Its product image is not a flat wrap.
- Kroger's 16 fl oz listing exposes front, back, left and right product views
  for UPC 0007084702901:
  <https://www.kroger.com/p/monstera-mango-loco-energy-drink-can/0007084702901>.
  This is useful for orientation and comparison, but its displayed copy and
  images are not a license to redistribute or reproduce the back-panel text.
- Monster Energy's Ireland page provides another official regional product
  view, but the product is branded JUICED and labeled 500 ml. It is not a
  substitute for the supplied JUICE can.

Searches found no official, openly licensed, high-resolution full-wrap artwork.
Paid tumbler wraps, fan designs, marketplace listings, and images with explicit
all-rights-reserved terms were excluded. The only raster used in the deliverable
is the user-provided reference; the other pages are cited as visual research,
not bundled assets. Public visibility does not establish a reuse license.

## Interim texture

`assets/textures/mango_loco_wrap.png` is a 4096 × 2048 flat label generated by
`scripts/prepare_artwork.py`. It deprojects the supplied image using a row-wise
silhouette radius profile and the near-orthographic mapping
`x = center + radius(z) * sin(theta)`. The visible front is placed at U≈0.267–0.733,
centered at U=0.5; it is intended for the project's front-facing -Y UV layout.
The central Monster claw and title remain sharp and legible. The unobserved
rear half is plain turquoise, not mirrored front art and not fabricated copy.

This remains a reconstruction from a rendered can image, not authentic press
art. The preparation script normalizes the observed saturated cyan background to
reduce duplicated studio shading. Some highlights, tonal shading in the art,
and the edge crop remain inherited from the source. The sides and rear, exact seam,
regulatory text, barcode, ingredients panel, and regional fine print have not
been reconstructed. Do not use this interim image as print-ready packaging or
as proof of exact package fidelity. Replace it with an appropriately licensed
full-wrap asset if one becomes available.

The material module loads and packs the PNG into Blender while retaining the
relative path `//../assets/textures/mango_loco_wrap.png`. The image can be replaced
without changing the material interface.
